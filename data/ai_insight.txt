RAG MARKETING KNOWLEDGE
-----------------------

When ROAS is low, marketers should review campaign targeting, creatives, landing pages, audience quality, and budget allocation.

ROAS measures revenue generated for every unit of advertising spend.
Higher ROAS generally indicates better advertising efficiency.

Budget should generally be shifted toward campaigns that consistently demonstrate strong ROAS and conversion performance.

A campaign should not be evaluated using a single KPI.
CTR, CPC, conversion rate, CPA, ROAS, spend, and revenue should be considered together.

When CTR decreases significantly, marketers should test different ad creatives, headlines, calls-to-action, and audience segments.
